# PrayerBlock

> **Your prayer times. Blocked automatically.**

---

## The Problem

You pray five times a day, but your Google Calendar has no idea.

Every time a colleague books a meeting at 1pm, you either miss Dhuhr or you're fumbling to check prayer times mid-flow. You've tried blocking the times manually — but prayer times shift by a few minutes every day with the sun, so the blocks become inaccurate within weeks.

There's no app that simply connects your location to your calendar and keeps your prayer times blocked automatically, accurately, and forever.

Until now.

---

## What PrayerBlock Does

1. **You set your location once.** City or postcode.
2. **PrayerBlock calculates your 5 daily prayer times** (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) — accurately, for your location, every day.
3. **It creates calendar blocks automatically** in your Google Calendar — 30 days ahead, on a rolling basis.
4. **You never touch it again.** Prayer times update automatically as the sun moves through the year.

---

## Who It's For

Muslim professionals in Western workplaces — doctors, engineers, lawyers, academics, finance professionals. People who use Google Calendar as their primary scheduling tool and need their prayer times protected without manual effort.
